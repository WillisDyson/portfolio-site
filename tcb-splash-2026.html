Logged-Out Homepage Redesign 2026 – TopCashback

Project introduction

A total redesign of TopCashback's Logged-Out Homepage, maximising visual appeal, usability and accessibility.

Note: I did not design this page besides providing Front End Dev and Accessibility guidance to help shape the design. Credit goes to the TopCashback Design/UX team for their work on the UI design.

My roles within the project:

  • Leading Front End Development in general and acted as the subject matter expert.
  • Governing accessibility and guaranteeing a high level of WCAG compliance.

Problems with the legacy page

  • Visually outdated and did not reflect the current brand identity.
  • Severely lacking in terms of WCAG compliance.
  • Lots of room for performance and SEO improvements.
  • Based on the above, and in line with some planned SSO (Single Sign-On) integrations, it was decided that we would completely update the legacy page visually.

The redesigned page

Improvements made

  • Implemented lots of motion and interactive elements to improve the feel and first impressions of the page overall.
  • Incorporated TipTop, the new brand mascot, to improve brand recognition and engagement.
  • Greatly improved accessibility and usability across the page for much higher levels of WCAG compliance.
  • Enhanced performance and SEO to ensure a better user experience and higher search engine rankings.
